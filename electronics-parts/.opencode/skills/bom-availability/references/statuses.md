# Availability statuses

AVAILABLE: enough quantity exists via at least one eligible offer.
PARTIAL: known stock exists but total confirmed quantity is insufficient.
ORDER_ONLY: can be ordered according to supplier data, but not enough current stock.
NOT_FOUND: queried sources returned no matching offer and did not fail.
UNKNOWN: failures/ambiguity make absence impossible to assert.
INVALID_BOM_ROW: missing/invalid exact MPN or quantity.
